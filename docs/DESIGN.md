# One family, three products

PeakTweaks, Crosshair Forge and MacroForge should look like they come from the
same maker without looking like copies of each other. The shared parts are the
family DNA. Each product then gets its own colour and its own motif.

## Family DNA (every app keeps these)

| Element | Rule |
|---|---|
| Material | True black (`#000`) window and sidebar; cards one step up (`#0A0A0C`) with a 1 px hairline (`#222228`); 16 px card radius, 8 px controls. |
| The "alive" signal | **Lime `#C6FF34` means on, ready or running, in every app.** Nothing else is lime. |
| Type | Exo 2 ExtraBold wordmark; Plus Jakarta Sans for everything else; Cascadia Mono for numbers and keys. Headlines are heavy and tight-tracked, body text is muted grey. |
| Wordmark | Two words in caps, the first bold white, the second medium grey: PEAK·TWEAKS, MACRO·FORGE. |
| Mark | A geometric shape in the product's colour with one lime element inside, on a black rounded tile. |
| Shell | Left sidebar on black: wordmark, small-caps group labels, a 3 px colour bar on the active item, a status card at the bottom. |
| Status | Never colour alone: every state is an icon plus a word. Red only for errors. |
| Copy | Plain words, no promises: measured numbers instead of "faster" or "boost". |

## Product signatures (where they differ)

| | PeakTweaks | Crosshair Forge | MacroForge |
|---|---|---|---|
| Colour | Violet `#7E3BED` | Violet (shared with PeakTweaks) | **Signal cyan `#22D3EE`** |
| Motif | Faceted diamonds (a peak) | The crosshair itself | **Signal traces**: key presses drawn as timing lines, like a logic analyser |
| Hero | Solid violet card with facets | Live crosshair stage | **Black card with a live signal trace and a glowing cyan edge** |
| Objects | Cards of settings | Crosshair previews | **Keycaps**: every key is drawn as a physical keycap |
| Texture | Fine diagonal lines | – | **Dot grid** (a key matrix) |
| Text on accent | White | White | **Black** (cyan is bright) |

Users can still pick any of the family accents in Settings; the signature
colour is the default.

## Sound (MacroForge)

Cues should be felt more than heard: short, soft and low in the mix,
like a key switch, never a beep. The default is "Soft click" at 30 %
volume. Volume and style are in Settings, with a test button and an off
switch.
